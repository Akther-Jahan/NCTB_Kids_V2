NCTB Kids - Mimi Animated Intro Patch

1. Stop Expo with Ctrl+C.
2. Copy the assets and src folders from this patch into your KIDApp_review project root.
3. When Windows asks, choose Replace the file in the destination for LessonPlayerScreen.tsx.
4. Do not replace or delete your .env file.
5. Run: npx expo start -c
6. Open Class 1 > Bangla > a chapter whose first activity is intro/story intro.

Expected result:
- Mimi enters with animation.
- Mimi gently floats.
- A Bengali dialogue card appears.
- Press "মিমির কথা শুনি" to hear Bengali speech.
- Press the existing "পরেরটি" button to continue the lesson.
