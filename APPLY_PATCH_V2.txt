NCTB Kids - Mimi Intro Patch V2

Why V2:
Supabase story_snippet activities with slides are converted to type "snippet", not "intro".
V2 renders Mimi for both "intro" and "snippet" activities.

IMPORTANT: Do not paste the outer folder NCTB_Kids_Mimi_Intro_Patch_v2 inside KIDApp_review.
Open the patch folder first, then copy only its "assets" and "src" folders into KIDApp_review.
Choose Replace/Merge when Windows asks.

Required final files:
KIDApp_review/assets/characters/mimi/waving.png
KIDApp_review/src/features/learning/components/MimiIntroActivity.tsx
KIDApp_review/src/features/learning/screens/LessonPlayerScreen.tsx

Verification in the KIDApp_review PowerShell terminal:
Test-Path .\assets\characters\mimi\waving.png
Test-Path .\src\features\learning\components\MimiIntroActivity.tsx
Select-String -Path .\src\features\learning\screens\LessonPlayerScreen.tsx -Pattern "MimiIntroActivity"

The first two commands must show True.
The last command must show the import and both render usages.

Then run:
npx expo start -c

Open Class 1 > Bangla > আমার পরিচয়.
If the lesson resumes at an old later step, press "আগে" until the first story step, or clear Expo Go app data and reopen.
